HELP  -  Offline Library
========================

This drive holds a library of books (including Wikipedia), street maps,
and an AI helper that answers questions from the books. Everything works
without the internet. Nothing is installed on your computer.


TO OPEN IT
----------
1. Plug the drive into a Windows computer (Windows 10 or 11).
2. Open the drive in File Explorer and double-click "Start"
   (the blue picture with a yellow book).
3. The Offline Library window opens in a few seconds. The books are
   ready after about half a minute. The AI needs longer: a minute or
   more, and several minutes on older computers. You can read and use
   the maps meanwhile.

If you double-click Start while the library is already open, the open
window comes to the front.

The same guide is inside the program: click the Help button.


THE BUTTONS AT THE TOP
----------------------
Home ........... the start page.
Back, Forward .. the arrows: the page you were on before, and back again.
Library ........ all the books, and a box to "Search all books" at once.
Maps ........... the street maps on this drive.
Ask ............ ask the AI a question.
Help ........... this guide.
Shut down ...... closes everything (see "To finish" below).

A small light next to the buttons shows whether the AI is starting,
ready, or not available. Point at it with the mouse to see why.


LIBRARY
-------
- Click a book to open it. Each book has its own search box at the top.
- To look in every book at once, use "Search all books" on the Library
  page. The first search can take a few seconds; later ones are quicker.
- Some books contain guides you can print, and videos you can play.


MAPS
----
- Click a map to open it. Drag to move. Use the mouse wheel, or the
  + and - buttons, to zoom.
- Use the map's search box to find a town or street.
- Some very old computers cannot draw the maps. The Maps page then says
  so. The Library and the AI still work.


ASK THE AI
----------
- Click Ask, type a question in plain words, and press Enter.
  For example: how do I purify water?
- The AI searches the books, then writes an answer. A full answer can
  take one or two minutes.
- Each answer lists its sources as numbers. Click a source to read the
  article it came from.
- You can ask a follow-up question. "New question" starts again.
  "Stop" stops a long answer.
- If the books do not cover your question, the AI says so.

THE AI CAN MAKE MISTAKES. IT IS NOT A DOCTOR. For anything about health
or safety, read the sources it shows, and get professional help
whenever you can.


BIGGER OR SMALLER TEXT
----------------------
Hold Ctrl and press + for bigger text, Ctrl and - for smaller,
and Ctrl and 0 to go back to normal.


TO FINISH
---------
1. Click "Shut down", then "Yes". Or close the window with the X in its
   corner.
2. Wait until the window has closed.
3. Remove the drive safely: click the drive picture near the clock,
   choose "Eject", then unplug it.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Nothing happens when you double-click Start:
  Wait 15 seconds; slow drives take longer. Then double-click it once
  more.

"Windows protected your PC":
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Your antivirus program blocks or removes Start:
  This is a false alarm; the program on this drive does not use the
  internet. Choose to allow or restore it in your antivirus program, or
  contact the person who gave you this drive.

The AI is not available:
  The light and the Ask page say why. The most common reason is a
  computer with less than 6 GB of memory. The Library and Maps still
  work.

Everything is slow:
  Plug the drive into a faster port. On many computers the faster ports
  are blue inside, or marked "SS".

The drive was unplugged while in use:
  Plug it back in, close the window, and double-click Start again.

A message says files are missing or the drive may be damaged:
  Contact the person who gave you this drive.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Private: nothing you read or ask leaves your computer, and your
  questions are not saved.
- Keep a second copy of this drive somewhere safe. Any drive can fail.
